6/23/2026: The IT Thing

Putting together this website was a bit more complicated than imagined. I've not had a personal site since geocities or maybe freewebs back in the early 2000s. My first was really just a bunch of Pokemon gifs and tinny final fantasy 8 midi playing in the background. It really was a different era online, and I'd say unequivocally a better one. 'Course getting old puts a rose-tinted filter on things but Google search used to WORK. There were also viruses everywhere, and I don't know if there are less now or I've just learned how to avoid them. I did download some sketchy stuff. At least one of my best friends is wise in the ways of coding and was able to give me enough of a crash course that I could be off and running. True tech wizard status. He tried to get me to try a game called The Farmer Was Replaced but beyond the first couple of code exercises even Python made my brain scrunch the wrong way. Maybe using GitHub as a host isn't ideal but for this single page it should be more than sufficient. The thing to wonder really is will I be able to maintain regular updates. I have this problem with being creative--- it's not that I can't per se, but my mind simply rebels any time I try to Maybe it's fear of others laughing at my creations. I do better when things are anonymized, like when I was engaging in an unhealthy amount of FFXIV roleplay. I mean that was practically nothing but creativity, but at least I had a layer of abstraction obscuring my true self.

Growing Older

My birthday always feel like a strange holiday. I've always had a fear of growing older that has only gotten more acute at the years go on. I suppose this is a normal feeling for some people---though it's not so much a fear of death from growing old as a fear of becoming less how I see myself in my head. It's a dysphoria thing, I guess. At least there's cake.
