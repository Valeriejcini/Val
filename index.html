7/12/2026: Getting Fursonal

Jess and I went to Anthrocon, the largest furry convention in the world. It's held every fourth of july weekend in Pittsburgh---an unexpected choice to be sure. I guess the midwest is just a hotspot for the furrier folks. Not that there weren't people from everywhere visiting. Jess and I saw license plates from everywhere when we were looking for parking.

Being not really furries ourselves, we decided to just spend one day there and Friday seemed as good a day as any. It was ridiculously hot(They would have to cancel the parade on Sunday) but at least we didn't have to park a ridiculous distance from the convention center. Signage inside was a little confusing, but after finally making our way into the vendor area we managed to snag some cool items. Some wall art, naturally, and a pair of cute ears for each of us to help blend in.

I realized I had never actually seen a fursuit in person and it was actually more impactful than I expected. The designs were varied and quality actually seemed very high. I especially liked the Protogens, which are a race of cyborgs with cute LED visors. Jess on the other hand was enamored with the more realistic costumes, and we went to a meetup of such to people watch.

Overall, despite not having any plan(we missed several panels due to misjudging the amount of time we would need to show up in advance) we really had a good time. So good that we are already planning on going next year and trying to put a bigger group together.

For myself, I'm planning on putting together a protogen costume. Guess whatever all those folks have is contagious.

6/23/2026: The IT Thing

Putting together this website was a bit more complicated than imagined. I've not had a personal site since geocities or maybe freewebs back in the early 2000s. My first was really just a bunch of Pokemon gifs and tinny final fantasy 8 midi playing in the background. It really was a different era online, and I'd say unequivocally a better one. 'Course getting old puts a rose-tinted filter on things but Google search used to WORK. There were also viruses everywhere, and I don't know if there are less now or I've just learned how to avoid them. I did download some sketchy stuff.

At least one of my best friends is wise in the ways of coding and was able to give me enough of a crash course that I could be off and running. True tech wizard status. He tried to get me to try a game called The Farmer Was Replaced but beyond the first couple of code exercises even Python made my brain scrunch the wrong way. Maybe using GitHub as a host isn't ideal but for this single page it should be more than sufficient.

The thing to wonder really is will I be able to maintain regular updates. I have this problem with being creative--- it's not that I can't per se, but my mind simply rebels any time I try to

Maybe it's fear of others laughing at my creations. I do better when things are anonymized, like when I was engaging in an unhealthy amount of FFXIV roleplay. I mean that was practically nothing but creativity, but at least I had a layer of abstraction obscuring my true self.

Growing Older

My birthday always feel like a strange holiday. I've always had a fear of growing older that has only gotten more acute at the years go on. I suppose this is a normal feeling for some people---though it's not so much a fear of death from growing old as a fear of becoming less how I see myself in my head. It's a dysphoria thing, I guess. At least there's cake.
